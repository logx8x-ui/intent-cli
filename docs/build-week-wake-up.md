# Wake Up And Record

1. Unlock the Mac and wait ten seconds. Intent is already running with the clean
   four-intention demo canvas.
2. Press `~`. If the graph is not centered, click the fit-all control beside the
   zoom percentage.
3. Open [`build-week-demo.md`](build-week-demo.md) on your phone or second device.
4. Turn on macOS Focus mode and close anything private.
5. Press `Cmd+Shift+5`, choose the full screen, select your microphone, and make
   a 20-second test. Listen to it.
6. Record the 2:49 script. Speak steadily; do not restart for one imperfect word.
7. Trim only the dead air at the beginning and end. Keep the export below 2:59.
8. Upload it to YouTube as **Public** and watch the uploaded copy once at 1x.
9. Paste answers from [`build-week-submission.md`](build-week-submission.md) into
   Devpost.
10. Run `/feedback` in the primary Codex task and paste the Session ID before
    submitting.

Do not spend the morning redesigning. A working, understandable story beats one
more feature.
