# Intent Build Week Demo

## The One-Sentence Story

Intent turns a vague promise to focus into a computer-enforced contract, and
GPT-5.6 makes that contract fast to build without taking control away from the
person.

## Recording Target

- **Final length:** 2:40 to 2:50. Never exceed 2:59.
- **Format:** 16:9 screen recording, readable at 1080p.
- **Audio:** your voice, close to the microphone, with no background music.
- **Pacing:** let each result appear before saying the next sentence.

## Word-For-Word Script And Shots

### 0:00-0:14 - Prove It First

**Screen:** Start on the clean Intent canvas. Click **Data Science**. Show the
allowed apps opening, then return to the overlay briefly.

**Say:**

> I click Data Science, and my entire Mac becomes data science. Intent opens
> what I need and makes everything unrelated unavailable until I finish.

### 0:14-0:31 - The Problem

**Screen:** Show the Data Science square, its app tiles, website markers, and
connected restriction nodes.

**Say:**

> I built Intent because I would open my computer to do one tiny task, then
> disappear into tabs and apps I never meant to use. Most focus tools ask me to
> resist distractions after they appear. Intent changes what my computer allows
> before I begin.

### 0:31-0:51 - Explain The Visual Language

**Screen:** Hover across one square, one circular restriction, and Instagram's
triangular friction. Do not open editors.

**Say:**

> Each square is an intention. The app tiles inside are what may run, and a
> browser's markers show its allowed sites. Circles are restrictions. Triangles
> add friction. Together they form a visible digital Odysseus contract.

### 0:51-1:23 - Build With GPT-5.6

**Screen:** Finish the active session with `Cmd+Shift+M`. In the AI bar enter:

`I write university assignments, research sources, study data science, and reply to important messages.`

Show the generated drafts and their editable app/site choices. Do not spend time
editing every draft.

**Say:**

> At the bottom I can describe the work in plain English. GPT-5.6 turns it into
> specific, reusable intention drafts using only apps actually installed on this
> Mac. I can inspect every app and website before saving. The AI proposes the
> environment; I stay in control.

### 1:23-1:55 - Enforce The Contract

**Screen:** Return to **Data Science** and start it. Use the custom `Cmd+Tab` to
show only allowed launched apps. In Firefox, try an unrelated site and show
Intent returning to an allowed page. Briefly show `Ctrl+Tab` exposing allowed
tabs only.

**Say:**

> When a session starts, the allowed apps and sites open automatically. Try to
> leave and Intent sends me back. Its native lock and browser guards enforce the
> same contract in macOS, Firefox, and Chrome. Even its app and tab switchers only
> expose what this intention allows.

### 1:55-2:10 - Schedule Future Intentions

**Screen:** Press `Cmd+Shift+M`, open Intent, then three-finger swipe to the
scheduler. Point to a scheduled card and its time controls.

**Say:**

> A second desktop turns intentions into appointments. I choose the day and
> time, and the environment can start when I planned it, before present-me gets
> to renegotiate.

### 2:10-2:37 - Build Week And Codex

**Screen:** Glide back to the graph. Show Timer, Cooldown, browser restriction,
then the AI bar. End on the centered canvas.

**Say:**

> Intent began as my personal command-line prototype. During Build Week, Codex
> helped me turn it into this native spatial app: the graph canvas, adaptive
> glass interface, cross-browser guards, scheduler, focused switchers, timers,
> release packaging, tests, and hosted AI service. GPT-5.6 powers the new builder
> with structured, validated drafts.

### 2:37-2:49 - Close

**Screen:** Rest on the centered words **Choose one thing. Let everything else
wait.**

**Say:**

> Intent is for anyone who opens a computer with one purpose and loses it.
> Choose one thing. Let everything else wait. Make a promise to your future self,
> and actually keep it.

## Before Recording

1. Run `./scripts/prepare-build-week-demo.sh` from the repository.
2. Turn on macOS Focus mode so no notification appears.
3. Close personal messages, unrelated browser tabs, and anything with private data.
4. Put Firefox and Chrome Browser Guard switches on.
5. Open a clean desktop behind Intent so the adaptive glass looks deliberate.
6. Confirm microphone input in `Cmd+Shift+5` > **Options**.
7. Record one 20-second test and listen to it before the real take.
8. Keep the script on another device, not on the recorded screen.
9. Do not add copyrighted music or third-party promotional footage.

## Rehearsal Checklist

- `~` shows and hides Intent.
- Data Science starts and `Cmd+Shift+M` always ends it.
- `Cmd+Tab` shows only allowed launched apps.
- `Ctrl+Tab` shows only allowed browser tabs.
- An unrelated Firefox URL is blocked while Data Science is active.
- Three-finger swipe reaches the scheduler.
- The AI prompt returns editable drafts.
- The final take is under 2:50 before upload.
